Our Story

Where Every Mile Becomes a Memory

We are storytellers, explorers and culture enthusiasts — passionate about sharing the beauty of India and the world with curious travellers everywhere.

Who We Are

Born from a Deep Love for Travel

Life_in a Sphere was born in 2022 from a single road trip through Rajasthan — three friends, one camera, and an insatiable curiosity about the world. What started as a personal journal grew into a full travel magazine trusted by over 50,000 explorers worldwide.

Our mission is simple: to make the beauty, history and culture of India — and the entire world — accessible to every curious soul. We write not just as tourists, but as storytellers who believe every destination has a soul worth knowing.

From the ancient ghats of Varanasi to the caldera cliffs of Santorini, from Ladakh's star-filled skies to Kerala's whispering canals — we bring you destinations as they truly are, complete with their complexity, beauty and wonder.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every story, guide and destination review is grounded in these principles.

🔍

Authentic Storytelling

We go beyond the tourist brochure. Every piece of content is researched in-person, with an honest eye for what makes each destination truly special — and what to watch out for.

🇮🇳

Celebrating India

India is our heartland. From the Himalayas to the Indian Ocean, from ancient temples to modern cities — we are devoted to showcasing every face of this extraordinary nation.

🌱

Responsible Tourism

We advocate for slow travel, local economy support and respect for cultural heritage. Every article considers the environmental and social impact of tourism recommendations.

📸

Visual Excellence

Photography is the soul of our storytelling. We pair every destination guide with stunning visuals that transport you there before you buy the ticket.

🤝

Community First

Our 50,000+ community of readers, contributors and travel enthusiasts are the heart of this magazine. We grow together, share knowledge and inspire each other.

📖

Deep Cultural Respect

We approach every culture, religion and tradition with curiosity and reverence. Our guides help travellers connect meaningfully with the communities they visit.

Our Focus

Deep Roots in Indian Tourism

India is a continent unto itself — 29 states, 8 Union Territories, 6 seasons, 22 official languages, and thousands of years of unbroken civilisation. No single lifetime is enough to explore it all, but we're determined to try.

Our India coverage goes beyond the Golden Triangle of Delhi, Agra and Jaipur. We explore the remote Buddhist monasteries of Ladakh, the tribal cultures of Chhattisgarh, the colonial architecture of Pondicherry, the coral reefs of Lakshadweep, and the living traditions of every corner of this remarkable country.
