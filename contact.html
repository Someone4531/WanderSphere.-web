FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I write a guest article for Life_in a Sphere?
Yes! We welcome passionate travel writers. Send us a brief pitch (2–3 paragraphs) about your article idea, your bio, and 2–3 sample links to your previous work at contribute@lifeinasphere.com
How do I submit my travel photos?
We publish reader photography in our Visual Journal section. Email your best 5–10 shots (minimum 2000px wide, JPG/PNG) along with location details and a short caption to photos@lifeinasphere.com
Do you offer brand collaborations or sponsored content?
Yes, we work with travel brands, hotels, airlines and tourism boards whose values align with authentic, responsible travel. Reach out at hello@lifeinasphere.com with "Collaboration" in the subject line.
Are your destination guides regularly updated?
We update major destination guides seasonally, especially for information like visa requirements, best travel seasons and pricing. Each guide shows its last-updated date at the top.
How can I support the magazine?
The best support is sharing our articles! You can also subscribe to our newsletter, follow us on social media, and recommend us to fellow travellers. We're an independent magazine powered by our community.
